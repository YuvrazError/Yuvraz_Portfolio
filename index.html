About

01

I studied software engineering, but I've spent my career in the room where the deal gets made — and where it has to keep working after it's signed.

My path started in the field — walking through 1,200+ households with JICA to understand how a gas meter actually gets used, not just how it was designed to be used. That habit of going to where the work happens has stayed with me: as a Key Account Manager, I still spend as much time with dealers, technical teams, and operations staff as I do in boardrooms.

Today I manage enterprise accounts spanning telecom infrastructure, FMCG field operations, NGO programs, and SaaS platforms — coordinating cross-functional teams, aligning stakeholders with very different incentives, and making sure a signed contract turns into a working system.

What drives me is simple: relationships compound, but only if someone owns the follow-through. I try to be that person — the one who shows up after the pitch, not just during it.

01 Field first. Numbers on a dashboard mean less than what I hear from a dealer or field technician.
02 Own the handoff. Most account failures happen at the seams between teams, I manage those seams deliberately.
03 Measure what matters. Every initiative I run is tied to a number I can defend in a room full of stakeholders.
